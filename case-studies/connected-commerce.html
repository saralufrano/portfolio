01 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

A digital sale with a manual handoff.

Customers could buy through partners, but could not directly book their attraction visit on those partner sites. Guests brought vouchers to box office to exchange. Staff tracked vouchers in spreadsheets and reconciled them monthly.

Partners also lacked configurable inventory allocations. That limited the business’s ability to decide how much capacity to make available through each channel and at which times.

02 / THE PRODUCT DECISION

Connect the booking, inventory, and operation.

I led direct API integrations with CityPASS and GetYourGuide. The integrations now handle booking workflows and reconciliation that previously required manual exchanges and spreadsheets.

Partner inventory caps and time-slot availability controls gave the business the ability to align distribution with its commercial strategy.

We evaluated intermediary services and chose direct connections based on cost and control. The goal was to own the capabilities and avoid adding another layer of vendor fees to the operating model.

03 / PAYMENTS IN THE REAL WORLD

The rules have to work at the counter.

I also led the Chase-to-US Bank integration and contactless-payment rollout across approximately 16 kiosks and six box office selling windows, covering technical integration, hardware deployment, and testing.

Refund and credit requirements had to account for the specific banking integration’s same-day void behavior, historical purchase prices, changing ticket prices, and which product or bundle combinations the business could actually sell.

A bundle change is a product-policy problem, too.

Determining what a customer can retain—and what should be refunded—requires a valid remaining product configuration and the appropriate historical pricing. I owned translating those business and payment constraints into requirements.

04 / WHAT CHANGED

Connected workflows and clearer control.

  • Customers can book directly through the integrated partner channels.
  • Integrations handle the monthly voucher reconciliation workflow.
  • Operators can configure partner allocations and time-slot availability.
  • Contactless payments are supported across the kiosk and box office rollout.
  • Refund and credit policies are expressed through explicit product requirements grounded in actual banking and selling constraints.

The result is a more connected operation across the customer-facing transaction, partner distribution, and frontline workflows.
