HOW I WORK

Start with
the business.
Work backward.

The roadmap should explain why the work matters—and what has to become possible before the next step can happen.

  1. 01

    Choose the outcome.

    Revenue, customer experience, and operating efficiency lead to different priorities. At Space Needle, the revenue goal put pricing flexibility first.

  2. 02

    Build the foundations.

    Dynamic pricing needed more than a pricing feature. I mapped the product, inventory, permissions, and channel capabilities needed to make it work.

  3. 03

    Make the tradeoff.

    Build when control and fit matter. Buy when the deadline wins. Use actual behavior and operating costs to decide what to stop supporting.

  4. 04

    Help the team make better calls.

    I review feature work and coach product contributors, business analysts, and a peer PM on priorities, requirements, roadmaps, and stakeholder communication.
