Why we built it.

Control Station was not built because the business needed another admin tool. It was built because the existing operating model had become too fragile, too manual, and too dependent on disconnected systems to support modern attraction commerce.

Before Control Station

  • Ticketing, pricing, discounts, sellers, and channel configuration were spread across brittle workflows and legacy tools.
  • Operational changes often required too much manual coordination, tribal knowledge, or engineering support.
  • Pricing strategy was hard to execute quickly across timed entry, premium demand periods, events, and partner channels.
  • Seller access, permissions, and partner configuration were difficult to manage cleanly at scale.
  • Kiosks, box office, web, and third-party sellers did not have a strong enough shared operating backbone.

After Control Station

  • A single configurable commerce platform for products, pricing, inventory, sellers, roles, discounts, bundles, and channels.
  • Operators could launch and manage business changes with less dependency on one-off technical work.
  • Dynamic pricing and upsell tools gave the business new revenue levers across high-demand inventory and checkout flows.
  • Role-based access and seller management created clearer governance across internal users and external partners.
  • The platform created a foundation for real-time APIs, improved reporting, kiosks, and future AI/ML pricing intelligence.
